Glowjar – Support

Need help, found a bug, or have a question about a purchase? Email meyildiz1994@gmail.com and we will get back to you as soon as we can. Please include your device model and iOS version.

Purchases

Restore purchases: open the Store (or Settings) in the game and tap "Restore Purchases".

Glowjar VIP is an auto-renewing monthly subscription. It renews automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Manage or cancel it any time in Settings › Apple ID › Subscriptions on your device.

Refunds for App Store purchases are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Progress

Your progress is stored on your device. Deleting the app deletes your progress.
